Verify the foundation

  • Open the public key-file route · fixed demo key
  • Inspect the service receipt and the actual GET or POST fields below.
  • Compare 200, 202, denied requests, rate limiting and failures.
  • Retry deliberately using the form. This demo does not run a scheduler or retry worker.

The key-file route runs locally; its configured canonical location is on example.com. This simulator does not prove real host ownership. Keep production submission controls behind your application's authorization.

A received notification is not proof that a page was crawled, indexed, or ranked. Crawlable HTML, correct canonical URLs, publication-aware sitemaps and real 404 responses still matter.
